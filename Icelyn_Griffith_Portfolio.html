AI-Based University Email Router

Email classification • Department routing • Model experimentation

Project overview

Created a simple AI email-classification model to identify the likely destination department for an incoming college or university message. The project explored using LLMs and AI-assisted development workflows in Google Colab.

Routing examples

  • Admissions: application status, enrollment, and admission questions.
  • Athletics: sports programs, teams, and athletic department inquiries.
  • IT: account access, technical issues, and campus technology support.

The goal is to reduce manual sorting and help messages reach the appropriate team more efficiently.
